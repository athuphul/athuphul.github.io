A bit about me

I'm a software engineer at Roblox, working on distributed data problems that power one of the world's largest social platforms.

I'm a poker nerd who can't stop applying bankroll management and shot-taking logic to real life :) including making big bets when the math is right. It's also why my friends can convince me to send it down a double black diamond on the slopes.

When I'm not writing code or at the felt, you'll find me ogling roadsters, obsessing over retrograde complications, or explaining why Brighton & Hove Albion and Indian cricket are objectively correct life choices. I also ski, play guitar and am currently figuring out which grip to use on a tennis racket.
